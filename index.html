ABOUT ME

Hi! I'm Samuele Maltauro, born in 2005, and online I go by Chamered — a nickname inspired by my scout totem: "camaleonte disponibile". If you’ve ever been a scout, you probably get the reference!

I'm a web developer, mainly focused on frontend development, where I bring ideas to life through clean, responsive, and user-friendly interfaces. I love creating digital experiences that are both functional and visually engaging.

Outside of coding, I enjoy video games, playing the ukulele, and hitting the court for a game of volleyball. I like to think of myself as curious and adaptable — always up for learning something new or diving into a creative challenge.

The color red was assigned to me as part of my totem, and while it's not my favorite, it's now a unique part of my identity.

Always curious, always evolving — just like a chameleon.
